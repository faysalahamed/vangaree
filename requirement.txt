MASTER PROJECT PROMPT: VANGARE.SITE
1. Project Overview
Build a production-ready, responsive, modern classified marketplace named Vangare.site.
Vangare is a Bangladesh-focused marketplace for damaged electronics, working spare parts, component exchanges, second-hand devices, and repair services.
The business concept is to connect people who own devices with different damaged components so they can exchange usable components, sell spare parts, purchase required components, or find repair services.
Example Business Scenario
Person A owns a Samsung Galaxy A52:
•	Display: Damaged.
•	Battery: Working.
•	Motherboard: Working.
•	Wants: A replacement display.
•	Offers: Working battery or device components.
Person B owns a Samsung Galaxy A52:
•	Display: Working.
•	Battery: Damaged.
•	Motherboard: Working.
•	Wants: A replacement battery.
•	Offers: Working display.
The system should automatically identify these two users as potential exchange partners.
The main competitive advantage of Vangare is its intelligent component-matching engine.

E. Intelligent Component Matching Engine
This is the most important module.
Implement a dedicated matching service.
Example:
Listing A:
•	Device: Samsung Galaxy A52.
•	Working: Battery, motherboard, camera.
•	Damaged: Display.
•	Needs: Display.
•	Offers: Battery or exchange.
Listing B:
•	Device: Samsung Galaxy A52.
•	Working: Display, motherboard, camera.
•	Damaged: Battery.
•	Needs: Battery.
•	Offers: Display or exchange.
The system should identify this reciprocal match.
Matching requirements:
1.	Compare exact brand.
2.	Compare exact device model.
3.	Compare model number and variant.
4.	Compare required components with available working components.
5.	Support reciprocal component exchange.
6.	Consider location proximity.
7.	Exclude inactive and expired advertisements.
8.	Exclude a user's own listings.
9.	Avoid duplicate matches.
10.	Update match status when a listing changes.
Suggested scoring:
•	Exact device model: 40 points.
•	Required component availability: 25 points.
•	Reciprocal exchange compatibility: 20 points.
•	Location proximity: 10 points.
•	Active and verified listings: 5 points.
Maximum: 100 points.
Important business rule:
Exact model and component compatibility must be hard eligibility checks. A high score must never compensate for an incompatible device.
Matching result should show:
•	Matched user.
•	Device model.
•	Available component.
•	Required component.
•	Location.
•	Compatibility explanation.
•	Match percentage.
•	Contact or chat button.
Support match statuses:
•	NEW.
•	VIEWED.
•	INTERESTED.
•	ACCEPTED.
•	REJECTED.
•	COMPLETED.
Allow users to express interest and negotiate.
Do not automatically complete an exchange.
